# The Orwen Region

The Orwen region lies around Lake Orwen, in a wide green valley. Its three towns are Brassfield, Kettleby and Marrow Point. Brassfield is the largest and sits at the north end of the lake. Kettleby is known for its market square and its autumn festival. Marrow Point is a small harbour town on the western shore. The towns are linked by the Orwen Valley Railway, the Orwen Bridge and the Lake Orwen ferry.
